Quick Tips

Always Draft the Pantry Early

Pantry gives Steps back when entered. Place it on a high-traffic path between the Entrance Hall and the East Wing.

Don't Waste Keys on Wooden Doors

Wooden doors unlock by other means. Save Keys for steel doors and chests with gem multipliers.

Read Every Note

Notes left in rooms aren't flavor. Many contain the exact codes you'll need for the Vault and Safe.

The Bedroom Is Optional

Going to bed ends the day — but only triggers when you choose. Don't draft a Bedroom too early or you'll be tempted to quit a strong run.

Track the Constellation

The night sky changes each day. Note the visible stars on day 1 — they're the key to the Observatory puzzle later.

Save the Lab for Mid-Run

The Lab converts items but consumes a turn. Use it when you have a clear conversion target, not as a panic option.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Blue Prince a roguelike? Yes — daily randomized room drafting with knowledge-based meta-progression.
How long to beat Blue Prince? 15–25 hours for Room 46; 30–40 hours for the True Ending.
What is Room 46? The 46th hidden room in Mt. Holly. Reaching it is the main goal.
Is the Vault code the same every run? Yes — 4558, fixed.
How many endings are there? Three: Standard, Forgotten Wing, and True Ending.
Is there a map? 9×5 grid with Entrance fixed at bottom-center. See the visual map.
