Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 12, 2026

This privacy policy explains how Blue Prince Guide ("we", "the site") handles information when you visit blue-prince.concaxu.top. We are an independent fan site about the game Blue Prince. We are not affiliated with Dogubomb or Raw Fury.

Information We Collect Directly

This site does not ask you to register, log in, submit forms, or provide personal information. We do not run our own analytics scripts, cookies, or tracking code that we control.

Third-Party Services

The site loads resources from third parties to function. These third parties may collect basic technical information (IP address, browser type, page visited) according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) — serves the typefaces used on this site. Google may log requests. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages — this site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs HTTP requests for security and abuse-prevention purposes. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.
  • Google AdSense (if enabled in the future) — may serve personalized advertisements using cookies. AdSense uses information about your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google's Ad Settings.

Cookies

We do not set cookies of our own. Third-party services listed above may set cookies in your browser. You can clear or block cookies through your browser settings without affecting your ability to read the guides on this site.

Children's Privacy

The content on this site is suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided information through one of the third-party services above, please consult that service's privacy policy.

Your Rights

If you are in the EU, UK, or another region with data protection laws (GDPR, CCPA), your rights to access, correct, or delete personal data are exercised through the third-party services above, not through us, since we do not maintain a database of users.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when third-party services change or when we add new features (such as enabling AdSense). The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent through the contact information listed on the About page.
