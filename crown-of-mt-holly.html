Crown of Mt. Holly — True Ending Item Guide

Updated May 25, 2026 · 10 min read · Full four-artifact route

The Crown of Mt. Holly is the single item that triggers Blue Prince's True Ending. It is not found — it is forged. Four artifacts feed the Crown Chamber inside Room 46's Antechamber, and only when all four are seated does the chamber crown you and roll the final cutscene. This guide covers each artifact's location, the forging order (it doesn't matter, but the route does), and the three mistakes that brick a Crown run.

Quick reference: Four artifacts — Sapphire Eye (Mine Layer 3), Iron Circlet (Blacksmith trade), Golden Iris (Greenhouse + day-cycle), Star Sigil (Observatory Pattern A). Combine in the Crown Chamber dais inside the Antechamber. Cutscene fires on the dais step.

Why the Crown Matters

Blue Prince has three endings. The first two — Standard (open Room 46) and Forgotten Wing (Parlor route) — fire when you complete a single puzzle and walk through a door. The True Ending is different: it gates behind a collection that touches every major puzzle system, which is why the Crown is essentially a meta-achievement that says "you've engaged with all of Mt. Holly."

Practical takeaway: do not chase the Crown on Day 1 of a fresh save. Get the Standard Ending first to unlock Antechamber access, then plan the Crown as a multi-run project.

The Four Artifacts at a Glance

1. The Sapphire Eye — Mine Layer 3

The deepest of the three Mine layers is gated behind the Pump Room's PSI puzzle. You will need:

  • Pump Room drafted at least once
  • Lever sequence L–R–M, then PSI dials set to 40 / 60 / 80 (see Pump Room guide)
  • Lantern in inventory — Layer 3 is dark; falling without one costs the run

Once dropped into Layer 3, walk south. The Sapphire Eye sits on a stone pedestal at the dead-end. Pick it up. Climb back out via the Layer 2 ladder — the south route does not have a return path.

2. The Iron Circlet — Blacksmith Trade

The Blacksmith's Hut is in the Outer Area, accessible only after the Forgotten Wing route is open (Day 7+ for most saves). You need:

  • Three Iron ingots. One drops from each successful Aquarius Pipes puzzle, so plan to do the puzzle three runs in a row.
  • 15 Coins for the smithing fee.

Hand the ingots and coins to the Blacksmith. The Circlet is yours at end of day. It does not occupy an inventory slot — the game treats Crown artifacts as a separate ledger.

3. The Golden Iris — Greenhouse Two-Day Bloom

The standard White Iris bloom in the Greenhouse unlocks +1 Gem/day. The Golden Iris seed only appears in the drawer the in-game day after a successful White Iris bloom. Plant order:

  1. Day N: Plant White Iris seed. Water in the order Left → Right → Center. Confirm bloom.
  2. Day N+1: Re-draft the Greenhouse. The Golden Iris seed sits in the drawer's bottom-right slot.
  3. Plant the Golden seed in the center pot only. Water with the Sun Lamp on (the lamp's lever is behind the seed shelf). Bloom takes one full day.
  4. Day N+2: Re-draft Greenhouse one more time. Pluck the Golden Iris.

This is why the Crown is a multi-run project — Greenhouse alone eats three drafts.

4. The Star Sigil — Observatory Pattern A

The Observatory's ledger has a margin column most players never notice. On Pattern A nights only (Day 1, 7, 13 — see Observatory page), tapping the focus knob a third time after the stamp prints a small star icon in the margin. That print is the Star Sigil. Take the page out of the ledger and it's yours.

If you miss the third click, you have to wait six in-game days for the next Pattern A night. This is the most common reason Crown runs stall.

Forging the Crown

The Crown Chamber is a small chamber north of the Antechamber dais. To reach it:

  1. Have all four artifacts in your Crown ledger.
  2. Enter the Antechamber (Room 46 must already be open).
  3. Walk to the north passage. With all four artifacts in hand, the wall slides open. (Without all four, it's a blank wall.)
  4. Step onto the dais inside. The chamber forges the Crown automatically. The True Ending cutscene fires immediately.
Order doesn't matter, but the Antechamber resets between runs. If you re-bed before stepping on the dais, you have to re-open Room 46 to come back. Always combine the same day you finish collecting.

Three Mistakes That Brick a Crown Run

  • Selling a Crown artifact at the Pawn Shop. They show up in the Pawn's inventory window. The shopkeeper accepts them — and they're gone forever from this save.
  • Mining without a Lantern. Falling into Layer 3 dark voids the Sapphire Eye for the rest of the run, and the Mine resets the next day.
  • Bedtime between collecting and forging. The Antechamber resets. Re-open Room 46 means another Day-7+ run just to get back inside.
