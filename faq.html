Blue Prince FAQ — 50+ Questions Answered

Updated May 25, 2026 · 18 min read · Sections marked spoiler-free / spoiler

The questions below are the ones the community keeps asking — on Reddit, Steam forums, and in our inbox. The "Gameplay Basics" and "Performance & Settings" sections are completely spoiler-free. From "Puzzles" onward, expect minor spoilers (room names, item effects). The "Lore & Endings" section contains major story spoilers and is explicitly marked.

Gameplay Basics (Spoiler-Free)

What is Blue Prince?

A first-person puzzle / room-drafting game where you explore Mt. Holly mansion, draft rooms to reshape its layout each day, and search for the legendary Room 46. Imagine "Roguelike Myst" with a draft mechanic.

Is it a roguelike?

Roguelike-lite. Each day the mansion regenerates, but your save file accumulates permanent perks. Closer to Slay the Spire's meta-progression than to NetHack's pure runs.

How long to finish?

Standard Ending: 8-15 hours. True Ending: 25-40 hours. 100% achievements: 60+ hours.

Is there combat?

No. Threats exist (Antechamber Watcher, Mine trap floors) but they're survived through preparation, not fights.

Can I play with mouse and keyboard or controller?

Both, fully. Mouse + keyboard preferred by speedrunners; controller preferred by reviewers for the relaxing pace.

Does it have a tutorial?

Yes, ~20 minutes covering core mechanics. Auto-triggers on a fresh save.

Can I skip the tutorial?

Not on first save. On subsequent saves of the same Steam/PSN account, the tutorial is skippable from the title screen.

Does the game ever end?

Reaching Room 46 triggers the Standard Ending and a soft-credits roll. The save continues — you can keep playing toward other endings.

What platforms is it on?

Steam (Windows/Mac/Linux), GOG, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series. No Switch port announced.

Is there cross-save?

Steam ↔ Steam Deck only. No cross-platform save.

Performance & Settings (Spoiler-Free)

Minimum specs?

i5-4570, 8GB RAM, GTX 1050 / RX 560, 12GB disk. Runs fine on Steam Deck at 60fps.

Are there accessibility options?

Yes. Subtitles, font scaling, colorblind palettes, reduced motion, mouse smoothing. The Chapel puzzle has audio-cue mode for colorblind players.

Why does my framerate drop in the Library?

The Library renders all 200+ books individually. v1.2.4 patched this — make sure you're on latest version.

Save corruption — what to do?

Steam Cloud is your friend. Roll back via Steam → Properties → Backups. The dev team also has a save-restore form on the support site.

How do I change resolution?

Title screen → Settings → Display. The game supports 1080p, 1440p, 4K, and ultrawide 21:9. The 32:9 ultra-ultrawide has UI clipping issues, fix pending.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Achievements sync next time you go online.

What is the day-night cycle's real-time length?

Each in-game day = ~12-20 real-time minutes depending on how much you explore. Sleeping fast-forwards to next morning instantly.

Does the game support cloud saves?

Steam Cloud yes. GOG Galaxy yes. PSN cloud yes. No Xbox cross-save with PC.

Puzzles (Minor Spoilers)

What's the Vault code?

4558. Derived from "DOOR" via a letter-position cipher. See full derivation.

How do I solve the Boiler Room?

Valve order: Middle → Right → Left. Each pressurizes the next. Full guide.

How do I beat the Billiard Room with only 3 shots?

Chain combos. Shot 1: 1+2 ball pocket. Shot 2: 3→4→5 chain. Shot 3: cluster break. Full guide.

Dartboard 180?

Three triple-20s. Buy steady-hand Coin from Commissary first. Full guide.

Observatory constellation chart?

12 visible constellations rotate by in-game season. Each maps to a bedside book. Full chart.

Parlor riddles?

SMOKE, ECHO, PIANO are the three main. Plus three Forgotten Wing variants. All 6 answers.

Laboratory formulas?

Seven safe. Iron+Salt+Coal (Coin), Mercury+Ash+Iron (Lockpick), Salt+Sulfur+Ash (Key), Honey+Mercury+Coal (Pantry), Brimstone+Iron+Sulfur (Fire Wand), Honey+Salt+Brimstone (Vault hint), Ash+Coal+Mercury (Step buff). Details.

Greenhouse White Iris?

Rose-Plot1, Lily-Plot2, Iris-Plot3. Water in order: T1 Plot2, T2 Plot3, T3 Plot1. Full guide.

Chapel bell sequence?

Read altar plaque first — it rotates daily. Then pull rope in the displayed order. Day rotation table.

Aquarius pipe routing?

11 segments. T-junction at grid center, branch to all three sinks. Optimal layout.

Pump Room PSI?

Lever order L→R→M, then gauges 40/60/80 PSI within 30 seconds. Full guide.

Can I redo a failed puzzle?

Yes on next draft of that room. Most puzzles re-roll their state on re-draft.

Items & Resources

What's the Pool Cue do?

Widens Billiard shot tolerance from 5 to 12 degrees. Holding it makes future Billiard rooms easy.

What's the Wrench?

Opens utility doors (Boiler, Pump, Maintenance) without a Key. One charge.

What's the Cross?

Death prevention. Consumes itself on lethal damage, restores you to 1 HP. Stacks to 3.

What's the Master Key?

Opens any one door, including Room 46. Single use unless Vault-forged (then permanent).

What's the Camera?

Take photo of any room. Photos are recallable from inventory. 25 Coins at Commissary.

How do I get more Keys?

Library payouts, Commissary purchases (8 Coins each), Lab Formula 3 perk, Wine Cellar drops. See Resource Economy.

How do I get more Coins?

Library payouts, Sapphires (sell at Pawn 6 Coins each), Lab Formula 1, Vault loot. See Shop Rooms.

How do I get more Steps?

Pantry, Garden bench, Step Elixir (Apothecary), Bedroom sleep. Bedroom resets Step pool to current cap.

What's the inventory limit?

8 default. Trophy Room expands to 11 max (one slot per 5 trophies displayed, max 3 expansions).

Endings & Lore (MAJOR SPOILERS)

Spoiler warning: Endings discussed in detail below. Skip this section for first-playthrough purity.

How many endings exist?

Five: Standard Heir, Forgotten Wing, Outer Wanderer, True Heir, NG+ Forever.

What's the Standard Ending?

Reach Room 46. Accept the inheritance. Credits roll. Save continues for other endings.

What's the True Ending?

Standard + Forgotten Wing + Outer in one save, then return to Room 46. Reveals the Architect's true motive.

What is Room 46 actually?

It's the Architect's living quarters — and his grave. The "inheritance" is the responsibility of maintaining Mt. Holly forever.

Who is the Architect?

Your uncle's ancestor, who built Mt. Holly as a self-sustaining puzzle for posterity. He's been dead since 1873 but his consciousness persists in Room 46.

What is the Forgotten Wing?

A prison built by the Architect to contain his "failed" experiment — a sibling whose name appears only in the Black Market basement note. The Forgotten Ending releases them.

Who is the Watcher in the Antechamber?

The Architect's eye. The puzzle is whether you can endure being seen long enough to claim Mt. Holly.

What is the NG+ Forever Ending?

True Ending in Permadeath mode unlocks. Reveals that Mt. Holly exists in a loop and your inheritance restarts the cycle. Final shot: you waking up on Day 1 as a child.

Is there a definitive "good" ending?

Subjective. True Ending is the most complete narrative. Outer is the most peaceful. Forgotten is the most rebellious. Forever is the most haunting.

Achievements

How many achievements total?

42. 34 visible + 8 hidden. Full list.

Can I 100% in one save?

Practically no. Speed Demon (under 30 days) and Forever (Permadeath True NG+) require separate saves.

Are there missable achievements?

Yes. The Letter, Star Reader, Cousin's Confession, Black Letter, Sanctum Found. Missables list.
