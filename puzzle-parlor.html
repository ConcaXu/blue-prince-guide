Blue Prince Parlor Riddle — All Answers

Updated May 21, 2026 · 7 min read · Spoiler: Forgotten Wing route

The Parlor is a single room with a velvet writing tablet, a bookshelf along the north wall, and a riddle inscribed in mirrored text. The tablet's letters render right-to-left, which trips up many players — they think the puzzle is some kind of cipher when really it's just a typography trick. Read it backwards, type the answer, and a hidden door behind the bookshelf opens. Beyond the door is the Forgotten Wing — three uncatalogued rooms holding the journal fragments needed for one of the game's three endings.

Quick answers: SMOKE · PIANO · FOOTSTEPS · ECHO · TOWEL · MAP. Match today's riddle to the table below and type it on the tablet.

The Mirrored Tablet

Riddle Pool — Every Answer

Riddle on TabletAnswerWhy
"What walks in shadow but is born of light?"SMOKESmoke is born from fire (light) and follows shadow.
"I have keys but no locks, space but no room."PIANOPiano keys; piano "space" means the bar gap between staves.
"The more you take, the more you leave behind."FOOTSTEPSEach step taken leaves another behind.
"I speak without a mouth and hear without ears."ECHOStandard riddle. Sound bouncing back.
"What gets wetter the more it dries?"TOWELA towel becomes wetter as it dries other things.
"I can show you a kingdom but cannot leave my paper."MAPMaps depict places they can never visit.
Wrong answers don't punish you. Unlike the Observatory or Boiler Room, the Parlor accepts unlimited retries. Type freely until the correct word triggers the bookshelf door.

How to Solve It Step-by-Step

  1. Walk to the writing tablet. It is in the center of the Parlor, draped in green velvet.
  2. Read the mirrored riddle. Either tilt your head, or just match it against the table above.
  3. Type the answer. The tablet accepts typed letters. The cursor displays mirrored too — typing "S-M-O-K-E" will display "EKOMS." Don't worry about it.
  4. Press confirm. The bookshelf along the north wall slides aside, revealing a corridor.
  5. Step through. You enter the Forgotten Wing.

The Forgotten Wing

Past the bookshelf lies a stretch of three rooms not catalogued in the all-rooms database. They are not draftable — they only exist behind the Parlor door, and they reset when you sleep. Each room contains one journal fragment:

  • Fragment 1 — "On the Architect." M. Holly's note about the architect who designed the mansion. Also references a "fourth child."
  • Fragment 2 — "On the Children." A sketch of three children plus a fourth crossed out in red ink.
  • Fragment 3 — "On the Forgotten." A short letter signed by the fourth child, addressed to no one.

Reading all three (you must enter the Wing on three separate days, since the Wing resets each sleep) triggers the Forgotten Wing ending — one of three endings in Blue Prince. See the endings guide for the full unlock chain.

Is the Parlor Required for Room 46?

No. The Parlor is fully optional for the standard Room 46 route. It is only required if you are pursuing the Forgotten Wing ending, which is a separate ending from Room 46.

EndingParlor Required?
Standard Ending (Room 46)No
Forgotten Wing EndingYes — all three fragments
True Ending (Crown of Mt. Holly)Optional, but unlocks faster

Common Mistakes

  • Treating the mirrored text as a cipher. It is not encrypted. Just read right-to-left.
  • Giving up after one wrong answer. The Parlor accepts unlimited tries. Cycle through the table.
  • Trying to enter the Forgotten Wing in one day. Each fragment requires a separate visit on a separate day, because the Wing resets at sleep.
  • Skipping the Parlor. It is optional for Room 46, but the Forgotten Wing ending unlocks an achievement and a free permanent Coin bonus (+5 starting Coins per run after completion).

Parlor FAQ

What is the answer to "shadow but born of light"? SMOKE.
Why is the text mirrored? Visual trick — the tablet renders right-to-left. No cipher, just mirrored.
Where does the hidden door go? The Forgotten Wing — three uncatalogued rooms with journal fragments.
Can I retry if I type wrong? Yes, unlimited retries. No penalty.
Is the Parlor required for Room 46? No. Required only for the Forgotten Wing ending.
