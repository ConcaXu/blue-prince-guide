Blue Prince Complete Guide Overview

Published May 25, 2026 · Updated June 2, 2026 · 15 min read · Comprehensive game guide

Blue Prince is a puzzle-roguelike that combines strategic room drafting, mansion exploration, and intricate puzzle-solving. This comprehensive guide overview provides an in-depth look at every aspect of the game, from core mechanics to advanced strategies. Whether you're a newcomer or an experienced player seeking to optimize your runs, this guide serves as your complete reference.

What You'll Learn: This overview covers the complete Blue Prince experience — from understanding the draft system and mansion layout to solving every puzzle and reaching the true ending. We break down game mechanics, resource management, strategic room selection, and provide detailed solutions for all major puzzles.

Understanding Blue Prince

Blue Prince is developed by Dogubomb and published by Raw Fury. The game combines roguelike elements with puzzle-solving and strategic decision-making. Each playthrough is unique due to randomized room layouts, but the core mechanics remain consistent. Players navigate a 5×9 mansion grid, manage limited resources, and work toward reaching Room 46 — the hidden 46th room that serves as the main objective.

The game's depth comes from its interconnected systems. Every decision — from which rooms to draft to how you allocate resources — impacts your ability to progress. Unlike traditional roguelikes that rely on combat, Blue Prince emphasizes puzzle-solving, resource economy, and strategic planning.

Core Game Mechanics

The Drafting System

The drafting system is Blue Prince's central mechanic. Each time you open a door, you're presented with three room options. Each room has an associated gem cost (0, 1, 2, 3, or 5 gems). Critically, the first room in any draw is always free — you only pay gems for the second or third option. This creates strategic tension: do you take the free room, or pay gems for a more valuable option?

Understanding drafting probability is essential for advanced play. Room rarity tiers (Commonplace, Standard, Unusual, Rare) appear with different frequencies depending on your current rank. Lower ranks favor common rooms, while higher ranks introduce rare rooms more frequently. This knowledge allows experienced players to anticipate which rooms are likely to appear and plan accordingly.

The Mansion Grid

The mansion is a fixed 5-column by 9-rank grid. You begin at the Entrance Hall (Rank 1, center column). The Antechamber is fixed at Rank 9 center. Every other room is drafted, meaning the mansion layout changes with each playthrough.

The grid structure creates natural progression zones:

  • Ranks 1–3: Early game. Mostly common rooms. Focus on basic resource generation and learning room mechanics.
  • Ranks 4–6: Mid game. Standard and unusual rooms appear. Resource economy becomes critical.
  • Ranks 7–9: Late game. Rare rooms dominate. Puzzle rooms, sanctum-tier content, and endgame preparation.
  • Rank 10: Room 46 only. The final objective.

Resource Management

Four primary resources drive Blue Prince's economy:

Steps represent your daily movement budget. You begin each day with 50 steps (or fewer in harder modes). Steps are consumed when moving between rooms. Careful route planning ensures you don't exhaust steps before reaching your objectives. Some rooms can refund steps, making them valuable for extended runs.

Gems are the currency for drafting. Earning gems is essential — the Mine, Vault, Parlor, and many puzzle rooms generate gems. Efficient gem generation allows you to draft premium rooms and access high-value content.

Keys unlock doors. Different key types open different door categories. Keys are scarce resources, and their allocation significantly impacts your route through the mansion. Strategic key usage separates efficient runs from inefficient ones.

Coins are used at shops and for specific puzzles. Some coin persists between days, while other coin resets. Understanding coin mechanics helps you plan purchases and puzzle solutions across multiple days.

Strategic Fundamentals

Room Prioritization

Not all rooms are equally valuable. Experienced players prioritize rooms that provide disproportionate value:

Pantry and Coat Check allow you to store items between days and refund steps. These rooms provide massive economy boosts and should be drafted early when possible.

Workshop crafts essential items like the Shovel, Burning Glass, and Detector Shovel. Access to crafting enables solutions to multiple puzzles and unlocks hidden content.

Library provides books needed for the A New Clue cipher, Final Exam, and lore. Books are critical for multiple puzzle solutions.

Drafting Studio permanently unlocks new room floor plans. Building a diverse room pool increases your options in future runs.

Power generation rooms like Boiler Room and Electric Eel Aquarium enable connected rooms through duct systems. These rooms unlock puzzle solutions and secret areas.

Economic Optimization

Successful Blue Prince players think in terms of resource conversion rates. Understanding which conversions are worthwhile separates casual players from optimizers:

  • When is it worth spending gems to draft a premium room versus taking a free common room?
  • Should you spend keys on optional puzzles, or save them for required doors?
  • Which rooms generate the best return on step investment?

These decisions compound across a run. Small optimizations in resource allocation lead to dramatically different outcomes.

Puzzle Solving

Blue Prince features numerous puzzles that require observation, logic, and creative thinking. Major puzzles include:

The Vault Puzzle requires decoding a cipher to find the code 4558. The solution involves finding letters hidden throughout the mansion that spell "DOOR," which maps to the numeric code through a substitution cipher.

The Billiard Room presents a ball-clearing puzzle requiring a specific sequence of shots. The solution involves understanding ball physics and planning a chain reaction.

The Parlor Riddles feature logic puzzles with answers like SMOKE, ECHO, and PIANO. These puzzles test observation and deductive reasoning.

The Constellation Chart requires matching telescope dial positions to bedside book illustrations. This puzzle combines observation with reference matching.

The Laboratory challenges players to discover seven stable element combinations that provide permanent save-file perks. Experimentation and note-taking are essential.

The Greenhouse requires a specific seed order and water sequence to grow the White Iris, providing a permanent +1 gem bonus.

Each puzzle rewards careful observation and note-taking. The game provides clues through in-room notes, books, and visual elements. Successful puzzle-solving requires patience and attention to detail.

The Path to Room 46

Room 46 is the primary objective. Reaching it requires:

  1. Progressing through Ranks 1–9, drafting rooms strategically.
  2. Solving required puzzles to obtain necessary items.
  3. Managing resources efficiently to maintain progression.
  4. Reaching the Antechamber at Rank 9 center.
  5. Obtaining the Basement Key from the Antechamber pillar.
  6. Using the Basement Key to access Room 46 on Rank 10.

Most players require 15–25 hours across multiple in-game days to reach Room 46. The journey involves learning room mechanics, understanding puzzle solutions, and developing strategic planning skills.

Advanced Content and Endings

Beyond Room 46, Blue Prince offers extensive endgame content:

The Antechamber serves as a hub for endgame content. It contains four doors leading to different areas and houses the pillar containing persistent items.

Multiple Endings provide different conclusions to your journey. The Standard Ending triggers upon reaching Room 46. The Forgotten Wing Ending involves a specific route through the Parlor. The True Ending requires obtaining the Crown of Mt. Holly and represents the deepest content.

Hidden Rooms and Secrets reward exploration and puzzle-solving. The Observatory chain, secret passages, and hidden areas contain valuable items and unique experiences.

Curse Mode offers optional hard mode gameplay with reduced steps and debuffs. Beating Room 46 in Curse Mode earns the Cursed Trophy.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

New players often make these errors:

  • Rushing Room 46 too early — Day 1 is reconnaissance. Build resources and knowledge before committing to the final push.
  • Wasting keys on optional doors — Save keys for required puzzles and critical paths.
  • Ignoring notes and books — In-game notes contain puzzle solutions. Read everything.
  • Poor step management — Plan your route to avoid exhausting steps mid-run.
  • Drafting inefficiently — Not all rooms are equally valuable. Prioritize high-value rooms.
  • Neglecting resource generation — Early gem and coin generation enables better options later.
