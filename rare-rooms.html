Blue Prince Rare Rooms — All 14 Ranked by Impact

Updated May 25, 2026 · 12 min read · Drop rates from datamine

The draft pool is weighted. Common rooms (Hallway, Bedroom, Bath) make up about 60% of every daily draw. Uncommon rooms (Library, Study, Pantry) fill another 25%. The remaining 15% is split across 14 rare-tier rooms — and these are the ones that decide whether your run becomes a story or a footnote. This page lists all 14, their exact draw rates (datamined from the v1.2.4 build), and the order to draft them in.

Quick scan: Top tier = Laboratory (1.8%), Vault (1.5%), Observatory (1.2%). Mid tier = Greenhouse, Antechamber, Aquarius, Pump Room. Low tier (still rare) = Chapel, Trophy Room, Conservatory, Music Room. Bottom rare = Atrium, Wine Cellar, Sanctum (0.4% — game's rarest room).

How Rarity Works in the Draft

Each day you draft three rooms from a hidden weighted pool. The pool is reshuffled each morning, but weights are constant:

  • Common: 60% combined weight across ~12 rooms.
  • Uncommon: 25% combined across ~10 rooms.
  • Rare: 15% combined across 14 rooms (this page).

The pool has anti-streak protection: if you haven't drawn a rare in 8 daily draws, the next draw has a +5% rare boost. This is why "I never get the Laboratory" players eventually get the Laboratory — the game smooths out streaks.

The 0.4% Sanctum

The Sanctum is the rarest room in the game. Datamine confirms 0.4% draw rate — about 1 in 250 daily draws. Most players never see it across an entire save file. What it does:

  • Single-use room. Once drafted on a save, removed from pool.
  • True Ending lever. Contains the third of three levers needed to unlock the True Ending sequence in the Antechamber.
  • If missed, gated forever? No. After Day 30, the Sanctum's 0.4% bumps to 5%. The game knows.
If the Sanctum appears in a draft, you take it. No matter what your other two draft choices are. The Sanctum is the most consequential single draft choice in the game.

Rare-Tier Draft Priority Order

When two rare rooms appear in the same draft (uncommon but happens roughly once per 12-day run), the priority order:

  1. Sanctum — always.
  2. Vault — if Day 3+ and you have ≥1 Key.
  3. Laboratory — if you haven't mixed Formula 3 (Key Draft) yet.
  4. Greenhouse — if you haven't bloomed the White Iris on this save file.
  5. Antechamber — if Day 5+ and inventory contains a Cross.
  6. Observatory — Day 1 only, otherwise mid-tier.
  7. Everything else — situational. Default to the one whose puzzle you can solve fastest.

The Anti-Streak Mechanic

The game tracks how many days since your last rare draw. After 8 consecutive draws without a rare, the next draw is weighted +5% toward rare. After 12 days, +10%. This is invisible in-game but consistently observed.

Practical effect: if you're on Day 7 with no rares yet, Day 8 has a noticeably elevated chance. Plan your day's Step budget knowing you might draft a rare and need extra Steps to navigate to it.

Rare Rooms FAQ

Can rare rooms appear on Day 1? Yes, with the same 15% combined chance. The Day 1 draft is not weighted differently. The Observatory on Day 1 is the best possible Day 1 draft.
Do drafted-but-not-built rare rooms persist? No. If you draft a rare room and don't place it, the room returns to the pool. You'll get another chance.
Do rare rooms re-spawn after being built? Most do. The Sanctum is the exception — single-use per save. Vault re-spawns but its rewards diminish (5 Sapphires first time, 2 thereafter).
Does the rare-tier list change in NG+? No. Same 14 rooms, same drop rates. The save-file permanent perks carry over but room weights do not.
