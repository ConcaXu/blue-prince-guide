About Blue Prince Guide

Updated May 12, 2026

What This Site Is

Blue Prince Guide is an independent fan site dedicated to the puzzle-roguelike Blue Prince. We publish walkthroughs, puzzle solutions, draft strategy, and the kind of long-form notes that help players go from confused to confident — and from confident to reaching Room 46.

The site launched in April 2026 after we noticed that the existing community resources were scattered across forum threads, video timestamps, and incomplete wikis. We wanted one place where the answer to "how do I get past the Vault?" was three sentences, not a 40-minute video.

What We Cover

  • A beginner's guide to the draft system, room economy, and the first ten days of play.
  • Solutions to every major puzzle — Vault, Boiler, Billiard, Parlor, Dartboard, Constellation, and the hidden rooms behind them.
  • The full Room 46 route, written by players who have cleared it.
  • Tier lists, opening moves, and small tactical notes that are too short for a guide of their own but matter in a run.

Who Writes It

The site is maintained by a small group of players (currently two of us) who have cleared Room 46 multiple times across save files. We are not employees of Dogubomb or Raw Fury, and no one at the studio has reviewed or endorsed our content.

When a contributor adds a section, we play through the relevant scenario at least twice before publishing. If the game receives a balance patch that changes a route, we update the affected guide — or we add a note explaining what changed and when.

Languages

The site is available in English and Simplified Chinese (简体中文). Translations are done by the same contributors who write the originals, so the Chinese pages aren't machine-translated and read like a guide written by a player who speaks the language.

Funding

The site is a hobby project. Hosting is free (GitHub Pages); the domain is paid by the maintainers personally. If we eventually display advertising via Google AdSense, the ads will not influence guide content. We have never received compensation from the game's publisher or any third party in exchange for editorial decisions.

Corrections and Feedback

If you find a guide that is wrong, out of date, or unclear, we genuinely want to know. The fastest way to reach us is by filing an issue on the project's GitHub repository or by emailing the contact listed below. We try to respond within a week.
