Blue Prince All Rooms List

Updated May 14, 2026 · 14 min read · Reference

Every room in the Blue Prince draft pool, with rarity, door count, placement cost, and effect. Use the in-page links to jump to a tier. The 46 total = 45 placeable + 1 hidden.

Legendary Rooms (Gold Border)

The rarest tier — usually 4–6 Gems to place, but among the strongest effects in the game.

Special / Hidden Rooms

  • Room 46 — Hidden. Only accessible after meeting all three conditions.
  • Antechamber — Unlocks after first Room 46 visit. Contains the Master Key.
  • The Forgotten Wing — Three rooms behind the Parlor's hidden door. Triggers an alt ending.
  • The Cellar (Mine layer 4) — Requires Brass Key + 12 Gems. Holds the Crown of Mt. Holly for the true ending.

All Rooms FAQ

How many rooms total? 45 placeable + 1 hidden Room 46 = 46. Antechamber adds to the pool post-Room-46.
Rarest room? Legendary tier (gold border): Vault, Mine, Observatory, Locksmith, Trophy Room, Antechamber.
Mandatory rooms for Room 46? Mine (Brass Key), Workshop (Telescope Lens), Vault, Observatory, and one of Den/Library/Office.
Best Step rooms? Pantry (+5 first entry), Kitchen (+3 per entry up to 9), Conservatory (+1 per turn inside).
